About Our Institution

A legacy of quality Catholic education

Our History

Founded in 1950, BCC has grown from a small school to one of the most respected educational institutions in Negros Occidental, serving thousands of students from Binalbagan and nearby towns.

Vision

We envision a community of transformed individuals, rooted in Catholic values, competent in skills, and committed to serve God and humanity.

Mission

To provide accessible, quality education that integrates faith, knowledge, and skills, forming students who are responsible citizens and faithful disciples.

Academic Programs

Complete education from basic to college level

Basic Education

Kindergarten, Elementary, Junior High School, Senior High School (STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS)

College Department

BS Information Technology, BS Business Admin, BS Criminology, BEEd, BSEd, BS Hospitality Management

Graduate School

Master of Arts in Education, Master in Business Administration — for professional growth and leadership

Admission Requirements

  • Report Card / Form 138 (Original & Photocopy)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • PSA Birth Certificate
  • 4 pcs. 2x2 ID Picture
  • Honorable Dismissal (for transferees)
